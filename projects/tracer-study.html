Tracer Study & Alumni Data Management

Role: Data Management Intern Company: Career Development Center, UIN Maulana Malik Ibrahim Malang Year: 2025 - 2026
Odoo Microsoft Office CDC IT Intern

Project Description

A data management initiative focused on maintaining the accessibility, accuracy, and validity of annual alumni tracer study records. The project involved organizing alumni data and optimizing reporting processes to support the university's alumni tracking activities.

Key Features & Impact

  • Alumni Data Management: Organizes and maintains annual alumni tracer study records to ensure structured data management.
  • Data Accessibility & Validation: Supports the accessibility and validity of alumni records through systematic data organization.
  • Annual Data Reporting: Facilitates the preparation and management of annual alumni tracer study reports.
  • Impact: Successfully managed more than 500 alumni records annually while maintaining data accessibility and validity.
